## Policy reconciliation

Later-class entry checks the new class and every current class's primary-ability minimum13, preserving alternatives and paired requirements. First-character creation receives no invented multiclass prerequisite. Reduced grants remain exactly class-owned. XP/PB use total level; features and prepared spells use class level. First-level HP applies only at total1. Shared HP/CON atomic behavior remains #429, reached through #968 and the own class adapters. Higher-level creation and optional feats after20 remain #2053/#2054 under #336.

Extra Attack does not stack between classes; a distinct feature may supply a larger count. Pact-weapon restrictions remain attached to Thirsting Blade. Alternative AC formulas are choices: for an unarmored/unshielded Barbarian/Monk with DEX2/CON4/WIS3, AC16 or AC15 is valid, AC19 is not. The rule does not prohibit acquiring both formulas. Their own armor/shield eligibility stays with their real feature owners.

One Spellcasting class uses its own slot table. Multiple Spellcasting classes use full Bard/Cleric/Druid/Sorcerer/Wizard contributions and rounded-up half Paladin/Ranger contributions, with all twenty combined rows retained. Preparation and ability remain independently class-bound. Ranger4/Sorcerer3 has caster5 slots4/3/2 while preparing only its own class-eligible spells. A higher combined slot does not unlock higher spell preparation. Cantrip scaling uses total level unless that spell says otherwise.

Warlock's Pact pool remains separate, with its own capacity, level and recovery. Qualified prepared spells can cross-cast in both source-authorized directions using the actual owned pool. Ordinary slots do not recover on a Short Rest. Item/free/created-slot channels and unknown effect resolvers remain separate. Closing a catalogue, class decision or this decision grants no production entitlement.
