At Bell Quay, Tamsin suspected that Orrel had stolen the brass tally. She confronted him, but he denied taking it, and she found no evidence against him.

Tamsin checked the counter and found the tally beneath her own folded map. She withdrew the accusation and apologized to Orrel. He accepted the apology but declined to guide her that evening. She left alone for the east stair; the mistake had cost her his help.
