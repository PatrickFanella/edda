Mara tried to cross the sluice at Greywash with a rope, but the knot slipped. The crossing failed, and the current carried the rope away. She remained on the near bank with Iven.

Mara then offered Iven two silver for a ferry. He refused the payment because his boat was damaged; she kept the coins. She chose to wait beside the sluice, where the water was still rising. Neither of them had crossed.
